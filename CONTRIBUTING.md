# Contributing — suggestions, fixes, and full updates welcome

The RUSE Mod Manager is built **with** the community. Suggestions, bug reports, small
improvements, and even full-blown changes are all welcome — and they're not just collected,
they feed **directly** into updates.

## How to contribute

Open a **[GitHub Issue](https://github.com/LittleGroove/RUSE-Mod-Manager/issues/new/choose)**.
That's it. Pick the **Bug report** or **Feature / suggestion** form and fill in what you can.

You don't need to word it perfectly. If something about your request is unclear, we'll
comment on the issue to ask what you mean — so keep an eye on it after posting.

## What helps us act fast

- **Screenshots.** Drag images straight into the issue. A picture of the problem (or of what
  you'd like) is worth a thousand words.
- **Which tab / area** you were in (Mod Manager, Convert, Mod Editor, Settings).
- **Steps to reproduce** a bug, even rough ones.
- **The version** of the Mod Manager you're on, and whether you're on **Public RUSE** or
  **RUSE Compat**.

## How submissions are handled

Every issue is read and taken seriously. We take all of it with a friendly grain of salt —
sometimes intent is hard to put into words — so don't be surprised if we follow up with a
question or two before making the change. Closed issues are archived, not lost.

Thank you for taking the time. It genuinely saves us a lot of work and makes the tool better
for everyone.
